隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 21 日

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于「饥荒食谱」（Starve Cookbook）iOS 应用。应用提供方以 App Store 产品页面列示的开发者为准。如有隐私问题，或需要处理你主动提供的信息，请联系 chenfei_0426@163.com。

2. 在设备上保存的数据

应用使用 iOS 本地应用存储保存收藏物品 ID、智能锅槽位中的食材，以及候选列表的显示偏好，用于下次打开应用时恢复状态。配方模拟、搜索与筛选均在设备上进行。这些输入与偏好不会发送给开发者。

3. 账号、跟踪和权限

应用无需注册，也没有用于保存用户数据的开发者服务器。当前版本不包含广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方分析 SDK，不请求定位、通讯录、照片、麦克风或相机权限。

4. 反馈与外部服务

在设置的反馈页面点按「复制」时，草稿会写入系统剪贴板，复制操作不会发送反馈。若你主动发送邮件，我们会使用你的邮箱、正文和随附文件答复及排查问题，保留期限以处理所需或法律要求为准。访问公开网页或打开外部链接时，目标网站及其托管方可能根据各自规则处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息等常规访问数据。App Store 下载及系统诊断由 Apple 根据你的设置和其政策处理。

5. 保存、删除与个人选择

本地偏好会保存到你修改它们，或删除应用及其本地数据为止。你可以在 App 内移除收藏、清空智能锅。系统备份及你已分享的副本需另行管理。对只保存在你设备上的数据，我们无法远程访问或删除。对你主动提供的信息，你可依据适用法律联系我们查询、更正或请求删除。

6. 政策更新

数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期。依法需要为新增处理取得同意时，会在处理前另行取得同意。

Starve Cookbook

Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Starve Cookbook iOS app (饥荒食谱). The provider is the developer identified on the App Store product page. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent us, email chenfei_0426@163.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves your favorite item IDs, Smart Pot ingredient slots, and candidate visibility preference in local iOS app storage. These values restore your choices when you open the app again. Recipe calculations, searches, and filters run on your device. The app does not transmit these inputs or choices to the developer.

3. Accounts, tracking, and permissions

The app does not require an account or use a developer-operated server to store user data. It does not include ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs and does not request access to location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

4. Feedback and external services

When you tap Copy in Settings feedback, your draft is placed on the system clipboard. Copying does not send your feedback. If you choose to email us, we use your address, message, and any attachments to respond and troubleshoot. We retain support correspondence only as needed for those purposes or as required by law. If you visit the public website or open an external link, the destination and its hosting provider may process ordinary access information, such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy terms. Apple handles App Store downloads and system diagnostics according to your settings and its policies.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local settings remain until you change them or delete the app and its local data. You can remove favorites and clear the Smart Pot from the app. System backups and copies you have shared are managed separately. We cannot remotely access or delete data that stays on your device. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you voluntarily sent, subject to applicable law.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will revise this policy and its date. Where the law requires consent for new processing, we will request it before that processing begins.
