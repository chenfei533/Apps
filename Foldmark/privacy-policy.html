1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Foldmark iOS app. The developer and operator is the app provider identified on its App Store product page ("we" or "us"). For privacy questions, corrections, deletion assistance, or complaints, contact chenfei_0426@163.com. Please do not send your complete fold history, backup files, or unrelated personal information. If troubleshooting requires supporting material, redact unrelated content first.

2. Information processed locally and its purposes

The app stores records you add or import on your device, including event identifiers, occurrence times, entry time zones, record sources, and request identifiers when present. These support deduplication, lifetime counts, history, and date-based statistics. The app also stores your earlier count estimate, installation date marker, onboarding progress, verification time, and appearance preferences to provide the corresponding features. Personal insights are calculated locally. Earlier estimates are excluded from daily statistics. Recording methods depend on your device, system version, and settings. Manual records use the time you enter; automatic recording saves events based on recording actions delivered by the system.

3. Information we do not collect

The app does not require registration, operate a developer-managed account or fold-history server, upload your fold history to us, or integrate advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not access location, contacts, microphone, camera, facial data, or fingerprint data for fold statistics, and does not use location access to maintain background counting. We do not sell or rent fold history or use it for targeted advertising.

4. File imports, exports, and system interactions

When you choose a backup to import, the app reads the selected file and validates and merges records on your device. Exported backups or CSV files include the relevant event times and counts; you select their destination through the system interface. Exported files should not be treated as encrypted and should be stored securely. Only when you choose image sharing does the app generate a statistics image locally and pass it to the system sharing or saving interface; it is not sent automatically. Declining an import or share does not prevent you from viewing existing local records.

5. Shortcuts and widgets

On supported devices and systems, you can configure a personal automation in Shortcuts for automatic recording. When a recording action runs, the app saves the event and updates counts locally without uploading your fold history to us. You can disable or delete the automation in Shortcuts. Device conditions or system execution limits may prevent some actions from being delivered; the app cannot recover those missing records. Widgets read locally stored summaries such as counts. The system schedules widget updates, which may appear later than in-app records. Refreshing a widget does not add a count. Feature availability depends on your app version, device, and system support.

6. Network access and third-party services

Local counting and statistics do not depend on a developer-operated server. When the app opens Shortcuts, the App Store, or an external legal document page, the relevant system or website provides that service. Web hosting providers may process necessary access logs, such as IP addresses and browser information, under their own privacy rules. The app does not attach fold history to these links. Apple handles downloads, payments, and diagnostics when you enable system diagnostic sharing; we may receive aggregated statistics or diagnostic information provided by Apple. When you choose to export files to cloud storage, email, or another app, your selected service processes that content under its own rules.

7. Storage, security, and deletion

Records remain in local app storage, with basic protection provided by system mechanisms such as sandboxing. This version does not offer in-app iCloud synchronization or developer-hosted cloud recovery, and does not claim independent end-to-end encryption for local files. You can delete individual records, clear recorded events, reset your estimate, or clear all data. Uninstalling may cause local history to be lost. System backups, exported files, and shared images may remain separately and must be deleted through the relevant service. Disabling an automation stops future records from that entry point without deleting history. Clearing app data does not disable system automations for you.

8. Your rights and choices

You can view, correct, add, and delete records in the app and export available backups. There is no online app account to close. We cannot remotely access, change, or recover content that remains on your device and has not been uploaded. You can contact the email address above with questions or personal information rights requests. After verifying the information necessary to handle the request, we will respond under applicable law; we will not request your password or complete backup. If you send a support email, we process your email address, message, and attachments to respond and troubleshoot, retain them only as needed for support or as required by law, and do not use them for advertising.

9. Minors and policy updates

The app is not specifically directed at children. Minors should use it with a guardian’s guidance. Where processing personal information of children under 14 requires consent, a guardian’s consent and assistance in managing that information are required. If data categories, purposes, recipients, or other significant processing practices change, we will explain the changes on the updated page and through appropriate app entry points. Where separate consent is legally required, we will obtain it separately rather than treating continued use as blanket consent. This is version 1.0, updated November 13, 2026, and applies from the availability of the corresponding version.

1. 适用范围和联系方式

本政策适用于FoldmarkiOS 应用。开发及运营主体以 App Store 产品页面列示的应用提供方为准，以下简称“我们”。个人信息相关咨询、更正、删除协助或投诉，请联系 chenfei_0426@163.com。请勿发送完整折叠历史、备份文件或其他无关个人信息；确需排查时，请先遮盖无关内容。

2. 本地处理的信息及用途

应用在设备上保存你添加或导入的记录，包括事件标识、发生时间、录入时区、记录来源，以及存在时的请求标识，用于去重、累计计数、历史查询和日期统计。应用还保存早期估算次数、安装日期标记、引导进度、验证时间和外观偏好，以提供对应功能。个人洞察在本地计算，早期估算不参与每日统计。记录方式取决于你的设备、系统版本和设置。手动记录按你输入的时间保存；使用自动记录时，应用依据系统送达的记录动作保存事件。

3. 我们不收集哪些数据

当前应用无需注册，不设置开发者运营的账号或折叠历史服务器，不向我们上传折叠历史，不集成广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方行为分析SDK。应用不为折叠统计获取定位、联系人、麦克风、相机、面容或指纹数据，也不通过定位维持后台计数。我们不出售、出租折叠历史或将其用于定向广告。

4. 文件导入导出和系统交互

你主动选择备份时，应用读取所选文件并在设备上校验、合并记录；导出备份或CSV时，文件包含相应事件时间和次数，保存位置由你通过系统界面选择。导出文件不应视为已加密，请自行妥善保管。当你主动使用图片分享功能时，应用才在设备上生成统计图片并交由系统分享或保存界面处理；不会自动发送。拒绝导入或分享不会影响已有本地记录的查看。

5. 快捷指令和小组件

在支持自动记录的设备和系统上，你可通过快捷指令设置个人自动化。记录动作执行时，应用在本机保存事件并更新次数，不向我们上传折叠历史。你可在快捷指令中停用或删除相关自动化。系统可能因设备状态或执行限制而未送达部分动作，应用无法恢复这些记录。使用小组件时，小组件读取本机保存的计数等汇总信息；显示更新由系统调度，可能晚于应用内记录，刷新本身不会增加次数。相关功能的可用性以你使用的应用版本、设备和系统支持情况为准。

6. 网络和第三方服务

本地计数与统计不依赖开发者服务器。应用打开快捷指令、App Store或外部协议网页时，由相关系统或网站提供服务；网页托管方可能处理IP地址、浏览器信息等必要访问记录，其处理适用各自隐私规则，应用不会把折叠历史附加到链接中。Apple负责下载、支付及在你启用系统诊断共享时的诊断处理；我们可能收到Apple提供的汇总统计或诊断信息。你主动向云盘、邮件或其他应用导出文件时，由你选择的服务按其规则处理相应内容。

7. 保存安全和删除

记录保存在本机应用存储中，由系统沙盒等机制提供基础保护。当前版本不提供应用内iCloud同步或开发者云端恢复，也不宣称对本地文件实施独立端到端加密。你可删除单条记录、清除实际记录、清零估算或清空数据。卸载可能导致本机历史丢失；系统备份、你导出的文件及已发送的图片可能独立保留，需要在相应服务中另行删除。关闭自动化只停止该入口未来记录，不删除历史；清空数据不会替你关闭系统自动化。

8. 你的权利和选择

你可在应用内查阅、更正、补录、删除记录并导出可用备份；无需办理本应用在线账号注销。对保存在你设备且未上传的内容，我们无法远程访问、修改或找回。你可通过上述邮箱咨询或提出个人信息权利请求，我们将在核实必要信息后依适用法律处理；不会要求你提供密码或完整备份。你主动发来支持邮件时，我们会为答复和排查处理邮箱地址、正文及附件，并仅在支持所需或法律要求期间保留，不用于广告。

9. 未成年人和政策更新

本应用不专门面向儿童。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，不满十四周岁儿童涉及个人信息处理时应取得监护人同意并由监护人协助管理。若数据类别、用途、接收方或其他重要处理方式变化，我们会在更新页面及适当的应用入口说明，依法需要另行同意时单独取得，不以继续使用一概替代。本文版本1.0，更新日期2026年11月13日，自对应版本提供之日起适用。
